How it's built (in plain terms)

📊 Owner dashboardorders, requests, alerts
  • No made-up facts. Prices, totals and availability come from the engine, never from the AI's memory.
  • Corrections change the real record. "Make it pepperoni on the whole pie" updates the stored order and bumps its version number.
  • Confirmation must match the latest read-back. If anything changes after the read-back, the agent reads it back again before accepting a "yes."
  • Never fake success. If the kitchen system fails, the order goes to staff and the caller hears the truth.
  • Safety first. Allergies, complaints and requests for a person go to staff. A gas smell gets a "leave and call 911" instruction right away.
  • No card numbers on the call, and no call recordings stored: just a structured outcome.

Scope This page is an original, self-contained simulation. It runs entirely in your browser with scripted lines and a small rules engine written for this demo. No AI model, phone call, network request or storage is used. The businesses, menu, prices and people are fictional. It is not SheerForce Voice production code, and it doesn't include the product's prompts, integrations, credentials or customer data.
